**The Maus gondola is designed for:** <br />
 - RC paramotor wings size 1.0m² to 1.5m²<br />
 - AUW from 0.9kg up to 1.7kg<br />
 - 2 or 3 cell lipos<br />
 - brushless motors size 28mm & 35mm diameter, length up to 36mm<br />
 - servos size standard 40x20 mm or smaller 35x15 mm<br />
<br /><br />

**chosing the right setup:** <br />
After 3D printing the MAUS gondola, you will have a pod with a weight arround 300 ot 400 grams.<br />
This is an important factor in calculating your motor/esc/lipo setup and the AUW for your Maus flightsystem. The AUW must match your RC paramotor wing.<br />
The Maus gondole has 4 servo bays. <br />
- 2 for steerling/braking lines
- 2 for speedbar lines. <br />
Speedbar is an optional feature that not all RC paramotor wings have. If you do use speedbar servos, use low profile servos.
By using low profile servors on the bottom the MAUS pod can accomodate battery up to 3 cell 4200Mah!<br />

**Prototype / tested setup:** <br />
- motor : HK 2836 2700Kv <br />
- prop : 9x4 folding <br />
- esc : HK 60Amp <br />
- servo : 2 x KST CM653 low profile <br />
- lipo : 4000Mah 2Cell (XT90) <br />
- AUW for prototype 1100 gram <br />
- Wing : Cefics CrossAlps 1.0 <br />

**Building instructions:** <br />
<br />
*Start with 3D printing all the parts* <br />
- Step 1: [follow instructions for the motor mount](1_MOTOR_MOUNT/README.md) <br />
- Step 2: [follow instructions for the servo mount](2_SERVO_MOUNT/README.md) <br />
- Step 3: [follow instructions for the mid section](3_MID_SECTION/README.md) <br />
- Step 4: [follow instructions for the back section](4_BACK_SECTION/README.md) <br />
- Step 5: [follow instructions for the front section](5_FRONT_SECTION/README.md) <br />
- Step 6: [check if you need to print some miscellaneous parts](6_MISC/README.md) <br />
<br />

*Mount all your electronics* <br />
 - Step 7: Connect the motor to the esc and insert esc and motor in the backsection of the MUAS gondola. <br />
 - Step 8: Insert the servos in the servo bays (mid section).<br />
 - Step 9: Connect servo arms to the servos.<br />
 - Step 10: Connect everything to your RX.<br />
 - Step 11: Insert the lipo.<br />
 - Step 12: Connect battery and power on your electronics, check that everything is working correctly.<br />
 - Step 13: Attach your RC paramotowing , brake lines , speedbar. [check info on hangbar](6_MISC/README.md) <br />
 - Step 14: Connect prop<br />
 - Step 15: Have a good flight.<br />
