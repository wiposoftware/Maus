# MAUS gondola/pod Front section

## Overview
The front of the MAUS gondola consists of three parts:
  - the cockpit
  - the nose cone
  - the canopy
<br/>
Currently, only an STL file is available for each component.

## Guide
### step 1 
Download the "frontsection.stl" file and use it in your 3D slicer program to print this part on a 3D printer.
I used 3 lines for wall thickness and 33% infill.

### step 2 
Use a good epoxy glue and glue it to the mid section part.<br/>
<img width="640" alt="Maus gondola cockpit, front section" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e69626b2-7131-4c14-b23d-bde06128405f" /><br/><br/>

### step 3
Download the "nose.stl" file and use it in your 3D slicer program to print this part on a 3D printer.
I used 3 lines for wall thickness and 33% infill.

### step 4
Use a good epoxy glue and glue it to the front section part.<br/>
<img width="622"  alt="Maus gondole nose cone, front section" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b5d07104-00f1-4557-a4e4-1ce470a91c3a" /><br/><br/>

### step 5
Use a good epoxy glue and glue in 2 magnets. max size 12x7mm and 2mm thikness
The magnets will hold the canopy in place during flight.<br/>
<img width="480" alt="Maus gondole cockpit magnets" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6912eb7b-9f32-41e9-b90b-fea9b9b0a21a" /><br/><br/>

### step 6
Download the "canopy.stl" file and use it in your 3D slicer program to print this part on a 3D printer.
I used 3 lines for wall thickness and 33% infill.

### step 7
Do NOT glue the canopy in place! :)
Test fit the canopy. Due to 3D printing tolerances, you may need to sand down certain sides slightly.
When the canopy fits perfectly you can add 2 magnets (size 12x7x2mm) to the canopy. You can glue the magnets in place or you can just use tape and wrap it arround the magnet holder.<br/>
